Yes. I would actually write the prompt differently for **Figma Make**. Figma Make works best when you describe the experience like you're briefing a senior product designer, rather than listing technical requirements.

The final design should be presentation-ready, prototype-ready, and visually polished rather than a low-fidelity wireframe.

## 💡 One tip for Figma Make

Instead of asking it to generate everything at once, ask it to build the project in **four passes**:

1. **Design System + Navigation**
2. **Homepage + Interactive Lens Experience**
3. **Remaining Pages (SEE, UNDERSTAND, SENSE, Technology, FAQ, Contact, Waitlist)**
4. **Prototype Connections + Animations + Banner Ads**

This usually produces a more complete and consistent result than a single giant request, while keeping the overall design coherent.
